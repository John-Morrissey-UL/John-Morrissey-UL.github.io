01 / ABOUT

About me

I work in educational technology at the University of Limerick, managing projects and driving innovation in our VLE, Brightspace (D2L), alongside the lecturers and administrators who use it every day.

Over summer 2026 I built and open-sourced six tools, the Summer Series, for jobs that Brightspace's own screens make slow: setting up groups and uploading grades in bulk, spotting assessment deadlines piling up across a programme, seeing who replies to whom in a discussion, and putting names to faces in a new class.

Each one runs on Brightspace's own APIs with the viewer's existing login, so it works on any Brightspace instance and needs nothing more than an HTML editor to install.

03 / APPROACH

How these are built

Every project follows the same constraints. They are what make the tools deployable by someone who has a course, an HTML editor, and no IT ticket.

One file, no build

Markup, styles, scripts and icons live in a single HTML file. Copy, paste into a Brightspace page or widget, save. Nothing to compile, nothing to host.

Your session, your permissions

Calls ride on the viewer's own Brightspace login. Nobody sees anything the platform wouldn't already show them, and no credentials are stored anywhere.

Portable across instances

API versions are auto-detected and course context comes from Brightspace's own replacement strings. Nothing is hard-coded to one institution.

Read-only unless asked

Reporting tools only ever issue GETs. The ones that do write — grades, groups, course status — say so plainly and preview the change first.
