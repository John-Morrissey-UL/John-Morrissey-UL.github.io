Discussion Network

See who's really talking.

Brightspace shows a discussion one thread at a time. This draws the whole board as a network, so you can see at a glance whether students are talking to each other or only to you. It names the students nobody replied to, and a slider replays the term week by week.

Deadline Radar

Every deadline.
One timeline.

Each module sets its own dates, so nobody sees when a programme piles its heaviest assessments into the same week. Deadline Radar puts them all on one timeline, weighted by how much of the grade each one is worth, and shows special-access extensions as the second deadline they really are.

Turns a classlist into a searchable grid of faces for a quick roll call, or a printable face sheet. Sort, filter by group or role, and jump straight to any student's progress.
